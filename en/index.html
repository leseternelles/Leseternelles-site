Corporate seminar · Equine coaching

Colas Suisse, a morning with the horses

Twenty employees of Colas Suisse, who came from Bern, took part in a horse-assisted coaching session, after a half-day devoted to artificial intelligence. Theme of the morning: emotional intelligence. The horse acts as a mirror of our inner states and responds immediately.

  • Better understand your communication style
  • Refine your interpersonal skills
  • Develop adaptability and leadership
  • Measure the effect of your emotions on others

Lasting performance rests as much on mastering tools as on self-awareness and the quality of human relationships.
